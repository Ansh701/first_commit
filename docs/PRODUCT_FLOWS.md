# Product flow status

The local product uses realistic synthetic fixtures and persistent browser adapters where external credentials are unavailable. Production interfaces use Cognito, PostgreSQL, private S3 access, and Razorpay test-mode webhooks without changing the screen model. Public discovery, organization profiles, causes, media, updates, and editorial pages cross a typed repository boundary so restricted workspace data is never used as a public response source.

Implemented end-to-end demo flows include:

- signup, email verification, login, recovery, reset, refresh, logout, archival, and restoration;
- ten-step autosaving organization onboarding with correction and resubmission;
- private verification-document review with reasons, internal notes, expiry, history, and organization decisions;
- donor, organization, and platform donation records with the 25-basis-point fee, fee coverage, captured progress, duplicate protection, refunds, transfers, receipts, QR codes, and CSV exports;
- money-independent item needs and pledges through acceptance, scheduling, and receipt;
- volunteer applications, events, follows, bookmarks, updates, corporate shortlists, matching records, team invitations, notifications, analytics, and exports.
- public organization discovery, approved-indicator profiles, cause detail, contribution context, item donation, volunteer/event entry points, vertical activity feed, public media galleries, editorial route families, and responsive light/dark/reduced-motion presentation.

All organizations, people, provider identifiers, payments, documents, images, activity, and decisions are synthetic. No fixture implies a customer relationship, affiliation, verified impact, real payment, tax deduction, or live AWS processing.

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Cookie Policy, Donation and Refund Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, and accessibility/security pages are hackathon drafts. They require professional legal, privacy, accessibility, tax, fundraising, and payments review before production.
